A business Conta Trepzy is the account relationship Trepzy establishes with a company — an entity that needs to receive international payments, hold funds in multiple currencies, or move value across borders. Unlike a personal account, a business account involves multiple people with different roles and scoped access. The account and its resources belong to the company itself, not to any individual on the team.

## Who This Is For

Business accounts serve companies like Aurora — organizations that operate across countries or currencies and need a unified place to manage inbound international payments, multi-currency holdings, and cross-border transfers. Any team member or representative interacts with the account in a defined capacity; no individual becomes the owner of the company's resources simply by having access to them.

## What the Company Must Provide

To open a business Conta Trepzy, the company needs to supply information about:

- **Who the company is** — its legal identity, registration, and basic organizational details.
- **What it does** — a description of its business activity, which informs compliance review.
- **Who represents it** — the people authorized to act on the company's behalf and confirm information or accept terms.
- **Who owns or controls it** — information about beneficial owners or controlling parties, as required by KYB verification.

<Note>
  A job title written in a form does not, by itself, prove that a person is authorized to represent the company. Formal authorization must be established and verified separately before that person can take actions that bind the company.
</Note>

## Who Can Act for the Company

Different people play different roles during and after account opening:

- **One person may fill in the initial data** — starting the onboarding process and providing the company's information. This does not make them the permanent decision-maker for the account.
- **Another may have formal authorization to represent the company** — confirming information, accepting terms, or approving operations, within a validated authorization.
- **Others may have scoped access** — viewing account activity, preparing operations, or approving specific actions based on the access they have been granted.

Depending on the nature of an operation, an additional approval may be required before value moves. For example, one team member may prepare a transfer while another authorized person must approve it before execution.

## How Company Resources Stay Separate

The account and everything in it belongs to the company — not to any individual team member, even those with significant access. This separation is structural:

- If a team member leaves, the account continues without interruption.
- Revoking a team member's access does not affect the account balance or any pending operations.
- A person's participation in a business account has no effect on their personal Conta Trepzy, and vice versa.

<Warning>
  Switching to a company account context in the app does not transfer personal funds into the business, and it does not grant the switching user any new permissions. Every action is evaluated within its own account context with the authorization applicable to that moment.
</Warning>

## The Account Opening Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Inform">
    Someone on the team fills in the company's identity, activity, and information about its representatives and beneficial owners.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit Documents">
    Supporting documents and authorizations are submitted to substantiate the information provided — company registration, identity documents for relevant individuals, and formal proof of authorization where required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verification (KYB)">
    Trepzy and its partner companies verify the submitted information. This process — known as **KYB** (*Know Your Business*) — may cover the company's legal existence, its activity, and the identity and authorization of the people associated with it. Verifying the company does not automatically verify every individual linked to it, and vice versa.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decision">
    Trepzy applies its criteria, resolves any inconsistencies, and decides what can be unlocked. A partner company providing a specific financial service may conduct its own independent review and reach its own decision.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Feature Unlocked">
    Once all conditions for a specific service are met — by the company, for that account, with verified representatives — the feature becomes available.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  A submitted document is not an approved document. A successful verification by a partner is not, on its own, Trepzy's approval. And Trepzy's approval does not automatically mean a partner financial institution has accepted the company for its specific service. Track each step independently.
</Tip>
