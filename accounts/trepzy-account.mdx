A Conta Trepzy is the primary relationship between a person or company and Trepzy — the container under which their resources, access, and financial services are organized. Every interaction with the Accounts product happens within the scope of a specific Conta Trepzy. Understanding this structure is essential for the Trepzy team, because context governs what actions are permitted, by whom, and against which resources.

## What a Conta Trepzy Is

When someone joins Trepzy, they open a Conta Trepzy — a formal relationship between that person (or company) and Trepzy. Under this relationship, the Accounts product becomes available, and any Global Accounts approved for that customer live inside it.

The Conta Trepzy is not itself a balance or a wallet. Think of it as the legal and operational context that makes financial services possible. Resources, permissions, and approvals are always evaluated within a specific Conta Trepzy, not across all accounts a person might touch.

<Note>
  Trepzy is **not a bank**. The financial services available within a Conta Trepzy are provided by regulated partner companies. Trepzy creates the experience, maintains the customer relationship, and coordinates the steps — but each underlying service has its own provider and its own approval chain.
</Note>

## One Person, Multiple Account Contexts

A single person can have more than one account context in Trepzy:

- **A personal Conta Trepzy** — belonging to them as an individual.
- **Participation in a company's Conta Trepzy** — where they act on behalf of the business, within whatever access they have been granted.

These two contexts are entirely separate. Resources in the personal Conta Trepzy belong to the individual. Resources in the company's Conta Trepzy belong to the company. The same login credentials do not merge those resources, and switching between account contexts in the app does not transfer value or grant any new permissions.

<Warning>
  Selecting another account context on screen is a navigation action only. It does **not** move money between accounts and does **not** elevate a team member's access level. Every action is evaluated within its own account context, with the authorization appropriate to that moment.
</Warning>

## Being Close to an Account Is Not the Same as Being Its Holder

Access to an account — even the ability to view its balance or prepare an operation — does not make a person the account holder. Holding an account means being the person or entity whose resources are organized under that Conta Trepzy, and whose authorization ultimately governs how those resources move.

This distinction matters when onboarding, when reviewing team permissions, and when a team member's access changes. Removing someone from a business account does not affect the account or its resources; those continue to belong to the company.

## The Global Account Lives Inside a Conta Trepzy

Within the Accounts product, a **Global Account** is a specific account organized around a single currency (for example, USD, EUR, or BRL). Opening a Global Account, obtaining receiving details for it, and seeing a deposit arrive in it are three separate events — each with its own state, its own conditions, and potentially its own partner approvals.

<Tip>
  When a customer reports that they "opened an account" but can't yet receive funds, check which of these three events has actually completed. Approval at one step does not automatically trigger the next.
</Tip>
