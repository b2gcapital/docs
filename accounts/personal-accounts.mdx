A personal Conta Trepzy is the account relationship Trepzy establishes with an individual — a person who wants to receive, hold, or move value across countries or currencies. This page explains who a personal account is for, how someone opens one, what the verification process involves, and what becomes available once that person is approved.

## Who This Is For

Personal accounts serve individuals like Ana — freelancers, contractors, or anyone who receives income from abroad, holds value in multiple currencies, or needs to move funds across borders. The personal Conta Trepzy is the starting point for accessing the Accounts product as an individual.

A person can have a personal Conta Trepzy and also participate in a company's Conta Trepzy. These are entirely separate contexts. Having a personal account does not grant any access to a company account, and participating in a company account does not change what a person can do with their personal resources.

## Opening a Personal Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Provide Required Information">
    The individual provides the personal information necessary to identify them — their name, identification details, and other data required by the verification process.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Identity Verification (KYC)">
    Trepzy and its partner companies verify the individual's identity. This process — commonly referred to as **KYC** (*Know Your Customer*) — may include confirming the identity document and checking that the person presenting it is the same person named in it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive an Approval Decision">
    Trepzy reviews the submitted information, resolves any inconsistencies, and decides what to unlock. A partner company providing a specific financial service may conduct its own review independently.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access Approved Features">
    Once approved, the individual gains access to the Accounts product and any Global Accounts approved for their customer profile. Additional features are unlocked on a per-person, per-service basis as conditions are met.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A submitted document is not an approved document. A positive result from a verification partner is not, by itself, Trepzy's approval. And Trepzy's approval does not automatically mean a partner company has accepted the customer for its specific service. These are distinct decisions at distinct steps.
</Note>

## How Personal Resources Stay Separate

Even if the same person logs into Trepzy and switches between a personal context and a company context, the funds never mix. This is a structural guarantee, not a setting:

- Personal money belongs to the individual and lives in their personal Conta Trepzy.
- A company's money belongs to the company and lives in the company's Conta Trepzy.
- The same login credentials do not transfer, merge, or re-attribute those resources.

Switching to a company account in the app is a navigation action. It does not create any new rights over the company's resources.

## Features Are Unlocked Per Person, Per Service

Approval is not a single gate that opens everything. Each feature — a specific currency, a specific type of operation, a specific Global Account — may have its own conditions, its own verification requirements, and its own partner approval.

<Warning>
  Access in one context does not carry over to another. A person who has been approved for a service as an individual is not automatically approved for the same service when acting on behalf of a company, and vice versa.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  If a feature is visible in the product but not yet usable for a given customer, check whether it is labeled **"In preparation"** in this knowledge base. Some features are shown in the interface while still being developed or rolled out for individual customers.
</Tip>

## What Is Available for Individual Customers

The specific currencies, operations, and Global Account types available to individual customers depend on what has been confirmed as active for that customer at any given time. The Accounts product and the Global Account features are the primary focus for personal account holders.

<Accordion title="Why some features may not be available yet">
  Trepzy builds and releases features progressively. A feature appearing in a presentation or on a screen does not mean it is currently available for all individual customers in all situations. Each operational capability is documented with its actual status — what has been decided, what is in preparation, and what has been confirmed in practice.
</Accordion>
