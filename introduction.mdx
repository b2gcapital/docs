This knowledge base is written for everyone on the Trepzy team. Whether you are joining the company for the first time or need to revisit how a specific part of the product works, this is where you start. You do not need a background in banking, digital currencies, or software engineering — every concept is explained when it first appears.

<Note>
  If a term looks unfamiliar at any point, open the [Glossary](/reference/glossary) and return to where you left off.
</Note>

## Status Labels

Throughout this knowledge base, features are marked with one of three labels so you always know the current state of a capability:

- **Available now** — an authorized person can use this feature under the stated conditions.
- **In preparation** — Trepzy is building or adjusting the feature; the complete journey is not yet ready.
- **Under study** — a possibility exists, but decisions about format, cost, and timing have not been made.
