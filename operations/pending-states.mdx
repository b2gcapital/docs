A pending state is not a failure. It means that an operation is in progress — being processed by Trepzy, awaiting a response from a partner, or waiting for information from the customer. The right response to a pending state is to track the original operation carefully, not to assume it failed and start over.

## Why operations enter a pending state

Operations can enter a pending state for several distinct reasons. Knowing the reason determines what should happen next.

<Accordion title="A partner response is delayed">
  Financial partners — including BlindPay and others — process operations on their own timelines. Network congestion, internal review queues, or banking-hours constraints can all slow a response. Trepzy continues monitoring; the customer typically does not need to do anything.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A manual review is required">
  Some operations trigger a compliance or risk review — for example, an identity check that could not be resolved automatically, or a transfer pattern that requires a human assessment. These reviews take time and cannot be bypassed.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The customer needs to provide additional information">
  If the operation depends on a document, a confirmation, or a piece of information that has not yet been supplied, the operation pauses until that input arrives. In this case, the customer must act — and Trepzy must make that clear.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The network is experiencing delays">
  Underlying payment networks and digital asset infrastructure can experience delays that are outside Trepzy's control. These resolve in their own time; duplicate operations will not speed them up and will create additional complications.
</Accordion>

## What the customer should see during a pending state

A pending state should never leave the customer in the dark. Trepzy must surface:

- **A meaningful status message** — not just "processing", but a description of what is being waited on (where this can be shared safely).
- **A clear next action**, if any is required from the customer. If no action is required, the customer should see that Trepzy is handling it.

If neither of these is visible, the pending state display needs attention.

## What NOT to do when an operation is pending

<Warning>
Do not repeat the operation. If a send, a deposit request, or any other operation is in a pending state, always check the status of the **original request** before taking any new action. Submitting the same operation a second time — especially for outgoing transfers — can result in the funds being sent twice, with no automatic recovery path.
</Warning>

## What the team must do

The Trepzy team is responsible for tracking the same request, not for assuming it failed and creating a new one. This applies both to internal investigations and to cases where a customer reports they "haven't seen a result yet."

Duplicate operations — particularly duplicate sends — can cause direct financial harm to customers. The financial risk of a duplicate is far greater than the inconvenience of waiting for a resolution.

When following up internally:

- Identify the operation by its unique identifier.
- Determine which party currently holds responsibility for moving it forward.
- Act on that specific operation — do not open a parallel one.

## Recovery paths

Once the cause of a pending state is identified, there is a clear path for each scenario.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Waiting for customer input" icon="user">
    The operation remains in a pending state with a clear prompt for the customer. No action from the team until the customer responds or a deadline is reached.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Waiting for a partner" icon="building">
    Trepzy monitors the partner's response. No customer action is typically needed. The team tracks the operation and escalates if the wait exceeds expected thresholds.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Operation ultimately fails" icon="circle-xmark">
    Trepzy communicates the failure clearly, explains the reason where possible, and provides explicit next steps — whether that is retrying with corrected information or contacting support.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Resolution confirmed" icon="circle-check">
    Once all parties have confirmed their part, Trepzy updates the operation state to reflect the actual outcome. The customer sees a final status with no further action required.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
Always resolve the original operation before initiating a new one for the same purpose. If the original operation's outcome is still unknown, treat it as potentially live. Starting a second operation before the first is resolved can produce duplicate results that are difficult or impossible to reverse.
</Warning>
