## Start Here

If you're new to Trepzy, follow this path to get up to speed quickly.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Understand the company">
    Read [What Is Trepzy](/what-is-trepzy) for the mission, the Accounts product focus, and the key partners involved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Learn core concepts">
    Work through [Accounts & Global Account](/concepts/accounts-and-global-account), [Money & Rails](/concepts/money-and-rails), and [Digital Assets & Networks](/concepts/digital-assets-and-networks) to build your conceptual foundation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Understand who can do what">
    Read [Accounts & Roles](/accounts/trepzy-account) to learn how personal and business accounts differ and how team permissions work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow an operation end-to-end">
    Review [Receiving Value](/operations/receiving-value), [Sending Value](/operations/sending-value), and [Tracking Operations](/operations/tracking-operations) to see how a full journey looks from the customer's perspective.
  </Step>
</Steps>
